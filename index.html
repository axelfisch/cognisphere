Explore your mind. Train your spectrum.

Cognisphere is a collection of elegant cognitive mini-games that estimate your mental profile — probabilistically, playfully, progressively.

Each session generates a snapshot across 9 active cognitive dimensions: logical reasoning, cognitive speed, working memory, pattern intuition, reality simulation, epistemic thinking, verbal analogies, sequence logic, and flexible categorization.

This is not a clinical IQ test. Results are probabilistic tendencies — a living map that sharpens the more you play.

About Cognisphere

Cognisphere is a cognitive mini-game app estimating your mental profile across multiple dimensions. An exploratory, probabilistic tool — not a clinical instrument.

V2 Beta covers 9 active domains with 9 mini-games. Spatial thinking, creativity, metacognition, emotional intelligence, empathy, and attention are planned for V3.

Scientific Limits

All scores are probabilistic estimates — not validated psychometric measurements. Does not replace professional cognitive assessment or medical diagnosis.

Privacy

All data stored locally. Nothing transmitted to any server.
