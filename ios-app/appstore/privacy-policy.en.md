# Cognisphere — Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

## Summary

Cognisphere does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data. The app works entirely offline.

## Data kept on your device

Your game scores, the history of your last 60 sessions and your settings (language, appearance, haptics) are stored only on your device, inside the app. They are deleted when you erase local data in the app or uninstall it.

## No account, no tracking

There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking. The app makes no network request while you play.

## Exports you choose to share

When you tap “Share JSON” or “Share PDF”, the app creates a file on your device and opens the iOS share sheet. The file goes only where you decide to send it. The temporary copy is removed afterwards.

## External links

The support link opens your mail app. A link to the online version of this policy opens in your browser. These services have their own policies.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.
